Hello! I am a second-year Master's student in CSE at the University of Michigan. I recieved my bachelor's of science at the University of Wisconsin-Madison, double majoring in Computer Science and Data Science with a certificate/minor in Game Design.

My research interests lie in multi-modal models, embodied agents, human-AI collaboration, and interpretability.
I am interested in how multi-modal models connect together and use information from different modalities, so they can better understand their environment to augment human abilities. I believe that effective agents must reason not only about the physical world, but also about the humans they interact with.

Currently I work in the SLED lab, advised by Joyce Chai, on research investigating how vision-language models (VLMs) establish common ground with human and autonomous partners, and the representational mechanisms behind this behavior.
